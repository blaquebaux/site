Equity / 001 / option

LONG CALL

The buyer pays a premium for the right—but not the obligation—to buy the underlying at the strike price. The position expresses a bullish view with limited downside and theoretically unlimited upside.

PAYOFF AT EXPIRY
max(ST − K, 0) − P

ST = terminal price
K = strike
P = premium

Expiry payoff

Net profit / loss
The premium creates the flat maximum-loss region. Above the strike, intrinsic value rises dollar-for-dollar; above 106, the trade is profitable after recovering the premium.
