What is Blaque Baux?

We build systems that turn uncertainty into structured edge.

Blaque Baux does not try to predict returns. The platform structures uncertainty around the edges that survive rigorous testing: diversification, trend, state, risk budgeting, and governed execution.

Provenance

BUILT BY CARTER WARRENS.

Blaque Baux is a quantitative research initiative and subsidiary of Carter Warrens, a boutique consulting firm co-founded by Kareem Williams.

Mr. Williams brings more than two decades of experience across global financial institutions, with work spanning capital markets, risk, data, regulatory systems, and financial technology. Blaque Baux continues that body of work independently: research the problem, challenge the assumptions, build the system, govern the risks, and document what survives.
