THE RESEARCH, MANIPULABLE.

Each study begins with the repository's actual thesis and reported finding, then exposes the idea through a restrained interactive surface. Reported metrics remain fixed to the research record; sliders and regime controls are used to explore the mechanism, not to manufacture new backtest claims.

HOW TO READ THE LABS

Finding is what the repository currently reports. Interaction lets you interrogate the mechanism behind that finding. Source remains authoritative for methodology, assumptions, code, and validation.

STATUS MATTERS

LIVE means the work has graduated into the governed spine or live path. VALIDATION means the idea has survived enough research to be studied here, but has not earned the production bar.
