Meta-sleeves / 14

CAPSTONES: THE ALLOCATION LAYER.

Capstones are meta-sleeves, themselves family sleeves one layer up. They form the allocation layer that sits atop the strategy sleeves and consumes their validated keepers as ingredients. They are alternatives, not additions (stacking them is redundant at +0.92 correlation). Seven are wired to governed live rails; two are research-only controls.

Null / graveyard / 51

THE NULL IS PART OF THE WORK.

These ideas did not clear the bar as standalone, buy-and-hold edges — which is not the same as worthless. A null is a standalone verdict, and many of these pay in context: as a diversifier that rises when the keepers fall, a positive-skew overlay that softens the book's tail, or a short-dose regime trade that works only when a leading signal says so. That portfolio question — does a sleeve the market would discard earn a slot in a balanced book? — is what the Capstones test, and the answer is often yes in spots, under the right conditions, in limited runs. The graveyard is the ingredient shelf, not a trash heap: read the finding before you discard it. Each entry also remains public so the failed thesis, constraint, or risk finding can inform the next test.
